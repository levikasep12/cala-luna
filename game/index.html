HOW TO PLAY

Follow the yellow waypoint. Finish story jobs to unlock the next one and earn cash. Spend cash at the gun shop (weapons, ammo, armor) and the garage (store the cars you steal). Side work pays too: taxi fares, package runs, street races and stunt jumps.

Heat brings the CLPD: 1 star patrols, 2 stars chases, 3 stars roadblocks, 4 stars the police helicopter, 5 stars SWAT. Break line of sight to cool down. Your crew helps: Marco repairs your ride, Ines wipes a star — press Q when their ability is ready.

Take over districts by clearing rival crews. Hold all five to unlock the finale payout. Progress saves automatically.
